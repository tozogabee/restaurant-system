# NNNN. Short title in the imperative

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Supersedes:** — <!-- or: 000N -->

## Context

What forced a decision. The constraints that were real at the time, including
the ones that later turn out to have been wrong — a reader needs to know what
was known.

## Decision

What was decided, in the active voice. One or two paragraphs.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table and why it lost. The most valuable part of the
document: it is what stops the same option being re-proposed in six months.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what it commits us to. Be honest
about the costs — a decision with no downside was not a decision.
